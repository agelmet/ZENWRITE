Mentoring & academic writing

If you want to learn to do it yourself, mentoring is the steadiest route. In one-to-one online sessions we work on your own text: how to build an argument, how to read an article, how to choose a statistical test, how to write a good abstract.

FAQ

Mentoring & academic writing: faq

How do the sessions work?

Online, by video call, at a time that suits you. We share the screen and work on your own files.

How many sessions are needed?

It depends on the goal: a single session on one topic, or a series covering the whole project.

Do you run group seminars?

Ask us about academic-writing or statistics seminars for small groups.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us the type of work, the length, the deadline and your department’s guide if there is one. The quote is free and without obligation.
