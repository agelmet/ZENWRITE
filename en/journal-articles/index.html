Peer-reviewed journals & conferences

Journal article support

Publishing in a peer-reviewed journal calls for a different kind of writing than a dissertation: concision, a clear contribution and a strict structure. We help you turn your research into an article, choose the right journal and respond to reviewers convincingly.

FAQ

Journal articles: faq

Can I turn my dissertation into an article?

Yes. It is one of the most common routes: we keep the substance of the research and rewrite it in the form and length of an article.

Do you help choose a journal?

Yes. We suggest journals that fit your topic and goal and check that they are not predatory.

Do you support conference papers?

Yes. Abstracts, full papers and posters for conferences.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us the type of work, the length, the deadline and your department’s guide if there is one. The quote is free and without obligation.
