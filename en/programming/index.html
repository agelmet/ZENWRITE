FAQ

Programming & labs: faq

Do you explain the code?

Yes. Every solution comes with comments and an explanation, so you can present it and answer questions.

Do you help with code for a dissertation?

Yes. Algorithm implementations, machine-learning experiments and simulations for dissertations and PhDs.

Which environment do you use?

Whatever the course requires: Jupyter, VS Code, Matlab, Visual Studio or IntelliJ.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us the type of work, the length, the deadline and your department’s guide if there is one. The quote is free and without obligation.
