Statistical analyses

The analyses we run

From a first descriptive picture to structural equation models — with the right test for every hypothesis.

  • Descriptive statistics

    Frequencies, means, standard deviations and charts.

  • Normality testing

    Kolmogorov–Smirnov, Shapiro–Wilk, histograms and Q–Q plots.

  • t-tests & ANOVA

    Comparing group means, with post-hoc tests.

  • χ² & non-parametric tests

    Mann–Whitney, Kruskal–Wallis, Wilcoxon, χ² of independence.

  • Pearson & Spearman correlation

    How strongly, and in which direction, two variables are related.

  • Linear & multiple regression

    Which factors predict the dependent variable.

  • Logistic regression

    The probability of an outcome and odds ratios.

  • Factor analysis EFA & CFA

    A questionnaire’s structure, loadings and fit indices.

  • Scale reliability

    Cronbach’s α, McDonald’s ω and item analysis.

  • SEM & PLS-SEM

    Structural equation models in AMOS and SmartPLS.

  • Mediation & moderation

    With Hayes’ PROCESS.

  • Time series & panel data

    Trends, seasonality and econometric models in R or Stata.

Which statistical analyses do you run?

Descriptive statistics, t-tests, ANOVA, χ² and non-parametric tests, correlation, linear and logistic regression, factor analysis, scale reliability, mediation, SEM and time series.

Which software do you use?

SPSS, R, Python, Stata, AMOS, SmartPLS and Jamovi — or whatever your department requires. If nothing is specified we suggest SPSS, the most widely used at Greek universities.

What do I receive from a statistical analysis?

APA-style tables and charts, interpretation for your results chapter and the analysis files (e.g. .sav, .spv or an R script).

Can you calculate the sample size?

Yes. With a power analysis (G*Power) we work out how many responses you need before you distribute the questionnaire.

What other support does ZenWrite offer?

Guidance on coursework, theses, dissertations and PhDs, proofreading, similarity checks, presentations, programming and academic-writing mentoring.

Which universities and departments?

All Greek and Cypriot universities, the Hellenic Open University, the Open University of Cyprus and colleges, plus universities abroad for work in English.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us the type of work, the length, the deadline and your department’s guide if there is one. The quote is free and without obligation.

What does the price of an analysis depend on?

The number of hypotheses and variables, the type of analyses, the deadline and whether interpretation text is needed.

Do you follow my department’s guide?

Yes. Structure, length, formatting and referencing always follow the guide you send us.

Is everything done online?

Yes. We work with students across Greece and Cyprus by email, phone or video call.

Is our work together confidential?

Yes. Your details and files are used only for the work we agreed.

Do you work with tight deadlines?

When there is enough time for quality work, yes. Tell us the deadline from the start and we will answer honestly.
