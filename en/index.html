The road of a thesis

Seven stops, one calm road

Every research project passes through the same stages. We know where it usually gets stuck — and we meet you there.

  1. 01

    Topic

    A topic you care about that fits your time.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    Aim, questions and method in a few clear pages.

  3. 03

    Literature

    The right sources, organised in Zotero or Mendeley.

  4. 04

    Methodology

    How the questions will be answered — and why.

  5. 05

    Analysis

    Data, tests, tables and interpretation.

  6. 06

    Writing & editing

    Coherence, style, references, formatting.

  7. 07

    Presentation

    Slides and a rehearsal before the committee.

What kind of support does ZenWrite offer?

Academic guidance on coursework, bachelor’s and master’s theses and PhDs, statistical analysis, proofreading, similarity checks, presentations, programming and academic-writing mentoring.

Which universities and departments?

All Greek and Cypriot universities, the Hellenic Open University, the Open University of Cyprus and colleges, plus universities abroad for work in English.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us the type of work, the length, the deadline and your department’s guide if there is one. The quote is free and without obligation.

What does the price depend on?

The type and level of the work, the length, the deadline and whether field research or statistical analysis is needed.

Do you follow my department’s guide?

Yes. Structure, length, formatting and referencing always follow the guide you send us.

Is everything done online?

Yes. We work with students across Greece and Cyprus by email, phone or video call.

Can I ask for help with just one part?

Yes. We can take on only the literature, only the statistical analysis or only the final edit.

Is our work together confidential?

Yes. Your details and files are used only for the work we agreed.

Do you work with tight deadlines?

When there is enough time for quality work, yes. Tell us the deadline from the start and we will answer honestly.
