Statistics for your thesis

In most research theses the toughest chapter is the results. We start from your research hypotheses, choose the right statistical tests and write the results chapter with you, with APA-style tables — so you understand it and can defend it in front of the committee.

FAQ

Thesis statistics: faq

Which statistical test does my thesis need?

It depends on your hypotheses and the type of variables. Usually a t-test, ANOVA, χ², correlation or regression — we decide together at the start.

Do you also write the results text?

Yes. We deliver the tables with text describing the findings per hypothesis, ready for you to adapt.

What if my hypotheses are not supported?

That is a finding too. We help you present it properly and discuss it against the literature.

What do I receive from a statistical analysis?

APA-style tables and charts, interpretation for your results chapter and the analysis files (e.g. .sav, .spv or an R script).
