Coursework support

Coursework is judged on structure, sources and the clarity of the argument. We help you understand what the brief asks for, find reliable sources and organise your text properly — for universities, the Hellenic Open University, the Open University of Cyprus and colleges.

What’s included

Coursework: what’s included

  1. 01

    Reading the brief

    What exactly is asked, how it is marked and how long it needs to be.

  2. 02

    Structure & outline

    An introduction, sections and conclusions that hold together.

  3. 03

    Reliable sources

    Greek and international literature from Google Scholar, Scopus and JSTOR.

  4. 04

    Literature reviews

    Synthesis and critical appraisal of existing research.

  5. 05

    Lab reports

    Method, results, tables and figures presented correctly.

  6. 06

    Referencing

    APA 7, Harvard or whatever style your department requires.

FAQ

Coursework: faq

Do you support Hellenic Open University and OUC assignments?

Yes. We know how modules and written assignments are structured and follow your module guidelines.

How long does a coursework assignment take?

It depends on length and complexity. We agree the timeline at the start, together with the quote.

Can I ask for help with just one part?

Yes. We can work only on the literature, the structure or the final edit of your assignment.

How do I get a quote?

Tell us the type of work, the length, the deadline and your department’s guide if there is one. The quote is free and without obligation.
